Paperlane.
A writing tool for slow, clear drafts

Writing, without the noise.

Paperlane is a plain-spaced editor for long-form work. No streaks, no dashboards begging for attention — just your words and a quiet place to shape them.

Start a draft How it works

Built for essays, chapters and the pieces that need room to breathe. One document at a time, from outline to final line.

Current issue: a new Paperlane pane ships with the next release.

01Distraction-free editor

One column of type on a warm paper background. Headings, margins and rhythm are handled, so your attention stays on the sentence.

02Editorial view

Watch your draft laid out like a printed page — with word counts, notes and structure tucked beside the text, never over it.

03Leaves your files alone

Work on local Markdown files that stay yours. Paperlane reads and writes plain text, so nothing is locked in.
